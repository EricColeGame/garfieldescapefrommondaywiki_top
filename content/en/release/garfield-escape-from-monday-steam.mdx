## What Is Garfield Escape from Monday Steam?

Garfield has appeared in racing games, party games, and movie tie-ins, but the escape-puzzle angle is a different bet. It trades spectacle for tight design, which historically has been a better fit for a character defined by low energy and high confidence.

If early player experience suggests the levels remix rather than repeat, that is the strongest signal the game will hold up.
