## How to Spot Fake Release Dates and Scams

| Early download or beta links | Common vector for malware and phishing | Only download from official storefronts |
| Paid "early access" keys from unofficial sellers | No legitimate pre-launch sale exists yet | Buy only from authorized retailers |
| Sites demanding login to view a date | Harvests credentials for no reason | Close the tab |
| Countdown timers with no publisher branding | Auto-generated, not official | Ignore and check the store page |
| Screenshots of private messages | Trivially fabricated | Wait for a public, first-party statement |

Community reports about fake listings are worth reading — many fan communities document scam domains quickly — but verify the scam itself before sharing links. Reposting a malicious URL to warn people can spread it further.
