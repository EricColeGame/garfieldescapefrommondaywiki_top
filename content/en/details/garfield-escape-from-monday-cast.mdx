## Garfield: The Character You Will Spend Most of Your Time With

Garfield is the anchor of the whole experience. Created by Jim Davis and introduced in 1978, he is defined by three things: he hates Mondays, he loves lasagna, and he would rather sleep than do almost anything else. That personality translates directly into how he plays.

Players report that Garfield functions as the all-rounder of the roster — reliable, easy to read, and forgiving for newcomers. If you are unsure who to invest your early resources in, the answer is almost always the cat himself.

- **Best for:** first-time players and anyone learning the core loop
- **Strengths:** balanced movement, forgiving timing, strong story presence
- **Weaknesses:** rarely the fastest or the flashiest option
- **Community consensus:** start here, branch out later

One useful habit: treat Garfield as your baseline. Once you know how a section feels with him, switching to a faster or more specialized character makes the difference obvious.

## Odie, Jon, and the Rest of the Supporting Cast

### Odie

Odie is the heart of the franchise and, in most versions of the Garfield: Escape from Monday cast, the most straightforward support character. He is not clever, but he is relentless. Players who enjoy a scrappier, higher-energy playstyle often gravitate toward him once they have the basics down.

### Jon Arbuckle

Jon serves as the story's connective tissue. He is the owner, the straight man, and the reason Garfield has a house to escape from in the first place. In-game, he tends to appear as a quest giver rather than a playable option — though community reports suggest some versions handle him differently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is in the Garfield: Escape from Monday cast?**

The core roster centers on Garfield, Odie, and Jon Arbuckle, with Nermal, Arlene, and Liz Wilson appearing in supporting and recurring roles. Exact availability varies by version and progression, but these are the characters players ask about most.

**Is Garfield the only playable character?**

In most versions he is the primary playable option, while other cast members function as support, quest givers, or story characters. Community reports suggest some builds expand the playable roster, so it is worth checking in-game menus early.

**Which character should I focus on first?**

Start with Garfield. He is the most forgiving all-rounder, and mastering him makes every other character easier to learn. Once you are comfortable, branch out to Odie for a faster, scrappier feel.

**Do I need to unlock every member of the Garfield: Escape from Monday cast to finish the story?**

Usually no. The main story is designed around the core trio, while the extended cast unlocks optional content, side routes, and collectibles. Completionists will want everyone; casual players can finish comfortably with the main group.
